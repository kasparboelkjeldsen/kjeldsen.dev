WHERE THE DIFFERENCE CAME FROM
- Architecture +1: ultracode put the post query in a
  service; high did the query in the block adapter.
- Content +1: ultracode's posts are about 470 and 490
  words; high's are about 300 each.
- Time: modeling and content took about the same in
  both runs (4 to 5 minutes). The extra time is in the
  Razor phase, 10:12 against 2:29, which includes the
  review workflow (about 3.5 minutes, 7 subagents) and
  the rebuild after it.
- The review found one low-severity bug, which was
  fixed. Neither extra point came from it.
