Why MCP is Awesome: Unlocking AI Integration

Published on September 29, 2026

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is revolutionizing how AI systems interact with tools and data. It provides a standardized way for large language models to understand and utilize external tools, databases, and services.

Key Benefits

  • Standardized Tool Access: MCP creates a common protocol for AI systems to discover and use tools.
  • Enhanced Automation: Seamlessly integrate AI capabilities into your existing workflows.
  • Content Management Integration: Tools like the Umbraco MCP server enable AI agents to directly manage content, media, and document types.
  • Multi-Agent Orchestration: Build complex workflows where multiple AI agents collaborate using MCP as the communication bridge.

With MCP, the possibilities are endless. Imagine AI agents that can automatically create content, manage media libraries, and orchestrate complex editorial workflows - all while maintaining your content governance standards.
