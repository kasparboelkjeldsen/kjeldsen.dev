Why MCP is awesome

A small blog about a protocol that lets AI agents work with real systems. Every page on this site was modeled, written and published through the Umbraco MCP server.

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that gives AI assistants a common way to call tools, read data and act inside the systems you already run. This blog collects short pieces on why that matters, with Umbraco as the running example.
