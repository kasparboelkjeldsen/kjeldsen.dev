MCP makes complexity feel approachable

A protocol becomes powerful when it helps more people do more with less ceremony.

MCP is awesome for a second reason: it lowers the cost of trying something new. A tool can publish a clear capability, and an assistant can discover it without a bespoke integration for every possible workflow.

That creates a healthy ecosystem. Editors get better experiences, developers keep ownership of their systems, and teams can compose small capabilities into workflows that feel tailored to the moment.

The result is not automation for its own sake. It is a more generous interface to the systems we already depend on.
