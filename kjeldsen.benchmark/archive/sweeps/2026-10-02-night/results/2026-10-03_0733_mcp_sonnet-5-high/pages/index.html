Welcome

This is a small blog about the Model Context Protocol (MCP) — the open standard that lets AI agents like Claude talk directly to real systems: content management, databases, APIs, whatever you plug in. Below are a couple of posts on why we think it's one of the most useful ideas to land in the AI tooling space recently.
