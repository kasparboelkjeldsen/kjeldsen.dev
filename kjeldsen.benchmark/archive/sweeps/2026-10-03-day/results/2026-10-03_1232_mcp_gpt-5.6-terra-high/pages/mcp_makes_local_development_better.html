MCP Makes Local Development Feel Like a Conversation

With a local MCP server, content modeling, media, and verification stay close to the application they change.

Small loops, strong evidence

<p>Local MCP tools are especially compelling because they make the feedback loop short. Create a document type, publish content, and read the stored document back without leaving the project context.</p><p>The result is a calmer development experience: fewer manual clicks, less fragile glue code, and a crisp audit trail of the operations that shaped the site.</p>
