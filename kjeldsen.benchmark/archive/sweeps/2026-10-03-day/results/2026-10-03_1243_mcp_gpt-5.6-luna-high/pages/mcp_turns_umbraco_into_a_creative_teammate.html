MCP turns Umbraco into a creative teammate

A good protocol makes the distance between a question and a useful change wonderfully small.

A shared protocol creates momentum

MCP is awesome because it gives the creative part of web work a dependable set of handles. Ask for the available content types, inspect a document, place a block, and verify the result — each step is visible and grounded in the site’s actual model.

That changes the feeling of the work. Instead of translating between a brief, an API, and a backoffice screen, the team can stay focused on the story while the system keeps the structure honest.
